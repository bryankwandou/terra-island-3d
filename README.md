# GPT-6.1 Terra — 10th Iteration Photorealistic World Engine

Real-time 3D tropical world simulation and interactive shader showcase built with Three.js, custom GLSL atmospheric and fluid shaders, subsurface coral reef caustics, wet-sand shoreline swash reflections, volumetric waterfall mist, and live split-screen iteration comparison.

## Interactive World Scenes

1. **01 / Sunset Atoll (`00:00–00:05`)**: Low skimming flight over crystal-clear coral lagoon waters with subsurface reef patches, Voronoi optical caustics, anisotropic solar glint, and 44 backlit pinnate coconut palms.
2. **02 / Cliffside Villas (`00:05–00:10`)**: Beach-level dolly along mirror-wet sand with rolling foamy shorebreak swash waves beside three cantilevered modern teak-and-travertine villas with warm 2700K architectural lighting.
3. **03 / Jungle Waterfall (`00:10–00:15`)**: Aerial flight over an undulating broadleaf rainforest and emergent palm canopy into a striated limestone canyon gorge with a roaring multi-chute waterfall, rising spray mist, and diagonal golden god-ray shafts.
4. **04 / Crescent Bay (`00:15–00:20`)**: High mountain-pass reveal over the jungle river valley out to layered coastal mountain headlands in golden aerial haze, a turquoise coral-reef crescent bay, and beachfront infinity-pool resort pavilions.
